# Contributing to Physica

Contributions are welcome, whether they fix a bug, add a feature, reproduce a paper, or improve the documentation.

## Licensing of contributions

By submitting a pull request you agree that your contribution is licensed under the license declared in [`LICENSE`](LICENSE), and that the maintainer may also distribute your contribution under any other license, including future versions of that license.

## Before you submit

1. Follow [`doc/StyleGuide.md`](doc/StyleGuide.md); consistency with the surrounding code takes precedence over personal preference.
2. Keep the change focused: one topic per pull request, and describe the motivation in the description.
